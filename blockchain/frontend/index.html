Decentralized Attendance Verification Architecture

How student attendance is recorded and protected against tampering:

1

Input Validation

Student ID, YYYY-MM-DD calendar date, and PRESENT/ABSENT status are validated client-side and verified server-side by attendanceValidator.js.

2

Duplicate Prevention

The contract computes keccak256(abi.encodePacked(studentId, date)). If the key exists, the transaction safely reverts to prevent double-marking.

3

Immutable Blockchain Storage

Transactions are mined on the local EVM blockchain via ethers.js. The smart contract emits an AttendanceMarked event with timestamp and signer address.

Transaction Guidelines

  • Immutability: Once recorded, an attendance entry cannot be edited or erased from the blockchain.
  • Duplicate Guard: Only one attendance record per student per date is permitted. Submitting again for the same date will be safely rejected.
  • Cryptographic Signature: Every record is cryptographically signed by the authorized backend wallet and timestamped by the EVM block.
