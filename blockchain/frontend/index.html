Subject-Wise Rules

  • Subject Uniqueness: A student can only have one record per subject per date. Submitting again for the exact same subject and date will be prevented.
  • Multi-Subject Permitted: A student can have different records for different subjects on the same date (e.g. CSE0917 on 2026-10-25: Java = PRESENT, DBMS = ABSENT).
  • Teacher Attribution: Your verified identity is permanently recorded on the blockchain with the entry.
